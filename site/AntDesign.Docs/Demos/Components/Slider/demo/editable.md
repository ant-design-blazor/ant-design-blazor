## zh-CN

启用 `Editable` 后，点击轨道增加节点；聚焦手柄并按 Delete 或 Backspace，或将手柄拖出滑块区域来删除节点。`MinCount` 与 `MaxCount` 限定节点数。

## en-US

With `Editable`, click the rail to add a handle; focus a handle and press Delete or Backspace, or drag it outside the slider to remove it. `MinCount` and `MaxCount` constrain the number of handles.
