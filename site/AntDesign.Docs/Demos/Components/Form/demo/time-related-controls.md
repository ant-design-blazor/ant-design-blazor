## zh-CN

在表单中组合日期、日期时间、月份、日期范围、日期时间范围和时间组件，并在提交时将值格式化为适合传输的字符串。

## en-US

Combine date, date-time, month, date-range, date-time-range, and time controls in a form. On submit, format their values as strings suitable for transport.
