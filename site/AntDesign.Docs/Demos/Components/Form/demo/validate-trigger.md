## zh-CN

Field A 在失焦时校验；Field B 在停止输入 1 秒后校验；Field C 按规则顺序检查长度，并显示对应反馈。

## en-US

Field A validates on blur, Field B validates one second after typing pauses, and Field C applies ordered length checks with corresponding feedback.
